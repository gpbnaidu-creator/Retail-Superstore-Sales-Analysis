## Retail Superstore Sales Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project using **Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI** to analyse retail sales performance, identify profitability challenges, and generate actionable business recommendations.

## Project Overview

This project analyses the Superstore retail dataset to understand business performance across regions, product categories, customer segments, and shipping methods.

The analysis combines data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL querying, business KPI development, and interactive Power BI visualisation to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights.

## Dataset Overview

- Original records: 9,994
- Columns: 24
- Data: Retail orders, sales, profit, products, customers, regions, and shipping information.
- Data preparation included duplicate checks, missing-value checks, and data type validation.

### 2. Product & Profitability Analysis

### 3. Customer & Shipping Insights

## Key Business Findings

1. **Regional Performance:** West was the highest-performing region, generating $725,457.82 in sales.
2. **Category Performance:** Technology generated the highest category sales at $836,154.03.
3. **Profitability Concern:** Furniture recorded a low profit margin of 2.49%.
4. **Top-Selling Sub-Category:** Phones generated $330,007.05 in sales.
5. **Highest-Profit Sub-Category:** Copiers generated $55,617.82 in profit.
6. **Loss-Making Sub-Categories:** Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies collectively recorded losses of $22,387.14.
7. **Regional Profitability:** Central recorded the lowest regional profit margin at 7.92%.

## Business Recommendations

- Review pricing and discount strategies for low-margin products.
- Investigate loss-making sub-categories, particularly Tables.
- Develop strategies to improve profitability in the Central region.
- Identify opportunities to expand high-margin product sales.
- Monitor regional and category-level profitability regularly.
